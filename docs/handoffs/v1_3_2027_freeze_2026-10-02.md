## What happened (2026-09-30 → 2026-10-01)

Full detail is in `docs/plans/v1_2_park_weather_plan.md` and
`docs/plans/v1_3_outcome_free_contact_plan.md`.

1. **Weather carry adjustment withdrawn (step 1).** `hit_distance_sc` already contains the
   weather: within venue, distance moves −1.45 ft per +0.01 kg/m³ of air density, which
   matches Nathan's ~3.3 ft per 10 °F. Script: `scripts/v1_2_hit_distance_check.py`.
2. **Per-venue station wind (step 2):** `mlb_luck_score.models.venue_wind_support`. 15
   venues supported, 5 contradicted (Oakland and Petco are robust), 9 insufficient. Oracle
   is supported.
3. **Wall heights reviewed:** `docs/reviews/park_wall_height_review_2026-10-01.md`, applied
   in `park_geometry_v12.py`. Gated-ball height coverage rose from 29% to 88%.
4. **`gated_geometry_v12` rejected** despite passing every automated criterion.
   `hit_distance_sc` is where the ball was fielded (glove, wall or seats; MLB Statcast
   glossary), so with the wall known it nearly labels the outcome: on gated balls, the sign
   of the margin alone classifies home runs correctly 84% of the time.
5. **`airball_geometry_v12b` (outcome-free, with geometry) rejected:** geometry *worsened* it
   (+0.005 log loss, 5 venues and 2024 regress). Untested hypothesis: geometry fingerprints
   the venue, as v0.3 did.
6. **v1.3 `outcome_free_v13` passed every development gate:** HGB on launch speed, launch
   angle, spray angle, batter side, `bb_type` and air density, for all batted balls, with no
   distance and no geometry. Log loss −0.201 vs `baseline_v02`; ECE 0.0147 → 0.0045; 2024
   −0.209. **Caveats, recorded in the plan:** the model-class change is bundled with
   dropping distance; density *worsened* the ablation (+0.004) and weather stays partly in
   the residual; removing spray angle worsens log loss by 0.10 (a possible fielding-location
   leak).
   2021–2023 have now been used three times; 2027 is the real test.
7. **2027 pre-registration approved and frozen:**
   `docs/plans/v1_3_2027_preregistration.md`.

## The 2027 test — what exists

- `prospective/v1_3_2027_config.py`: start date 2027-03-24 (MLB.com, 2026-07-16); **end date
  deliberately unset**, which blocks the run; guards.
- `prospective/v1_3_2027_ingestion.py`: one download into `data/prospective/2027/v1_3/`, plus
  the venue and weather joins.
- `prospective/run_v1_3_2027_comparison.py`: the only reader of 2027 data. Guards run first;
  it trains v1.1, v1.3 and the density-free variant on 2021–2023, scores 2027, and writes
  `outputs/prospective/v1_3/comparison_2027.json` once.
- **Primary endpoint:** paired log loss, v1.3 − v1.1, game-clustered, 500 replicates, seed 42.
  - Confirmed: CI entirely below 0, and calibration, venues and directions all pass.
  - Not confirmed: CI entirely above 0, or any gate fails.
  - Inconclusive: below 60,000 batted balls, or the CI straddles 0.
  - Density test: if it isn't supported, the next version drops density.
- **Accepted gap:** the Athletics' 2027 parks (Sutter Health Park 2529, Las Vegas Ballpark
  5355) have no station in the frozen venue table, so about 3% of batted balls have missing
  density.
