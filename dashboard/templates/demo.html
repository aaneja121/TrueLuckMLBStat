Teaching, not evidence. The page is one lede line, then two real batted balls that play themselves out stage by stage, then an instrument the reader drives. Everything that used to sit between the lede and the first card (a two-line subtitle, then a six-line callout between the cards and the simulator) is either gone or moved below the thing it describes. Where a quantity here is the same quantity the play page shows, it is drawn the same way: outcome probabilities are a neutral bar against a full-width track with the recorded result marked in text (Phase 6), and the Contact Luck payoff is a signed numeral with a word under it. What is NOT borrowed is the run-value gap figure. The demo's examples are 2024 fixture plays and are not in the published `run_value` domain, so drawing them on that axis would assert an alignment that is not true. The gap is stated as two labelled values and a signed difference instead. The two cards are one of the three admissible card cases (docs/design/layout.md: "a repeated instance of one object type in a set the reader scans across", which names /demo/'s two compared plays), and the simulator is another (an interactive instrument with a defined input surface).
