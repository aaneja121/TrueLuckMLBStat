These two plays are real, individual batted balls from the 2024 MLB season, scored with the same frozen Contact Luck model used throughout this site (see the recorded model configuration in the methodology page's technical detail). They were chosen to illustrate two clear, contrasting cases — they are not a representative sample of a typical batted ball, and a single play like either of these is never treated as its own judgment; it only ever contributes to a player's season-long total.
