How to read this

The total above is exact for the batted balls listed, given the scoring model it is measured with. It counts what those outcomes were worth against what the contact itself predicted, and nothing else — it is not an earned-run average, not a quality measure, and not a projection. A pitcher with more batted balls has more opportunities to accumulate a total in either direction, which is why the batted-ball count is printed beside it.

{{ p.role_label }} describes observed usage over this season — {{ p.appearances }} {{ p.appearances_noun }}{% if p.bbe_per_appearance and not p.single_play_season %} at {{ p.bbe_per_appearance }} batted balls each{% endif %} — and nothing more. This data carries no roster role.
