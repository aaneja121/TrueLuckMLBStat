The route's job is one path: hitter -> their published batted balls -> one play. So the picker is the first thing on the page and the results sit directly under it. The showcase follows the results rather than leading the page: with no hitter chosen it is the first substantive content a visitor meets, and once a hitter IS chosen it is below their plays, which is where an editorial sidebar belongs. That ordering needs no JavaScript -- DOM order, visual order and tab order are the same. Every play mark on this page is drawn on the ONE snapshot-level `run_value` domain computed in `dashboard/build.py` from every published play, with zero at the shared `--cl-zero`. It is not the leaderboard's `league_per_100` domain and does not pretend to be: the axis prints its own ticks and its own unit, which is the anti-fake-alignment guard (docs/design/dataviz.md). #}
