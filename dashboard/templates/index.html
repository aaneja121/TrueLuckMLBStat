Contact Luck Leaderboard

Contact Luck compares what a batted ball was expected to be worth with the run value of what actually happened. Summed across a player's eligible batted balls and normalized to Runs / 100, +3.0 means a hitter's realized outcomes were worth roughly three more runs per 100 eligible batted balls than expected. The interval shows the uncertainty around that estimate — Contact Luck describes what happened, retrospectively; it is not a projection of future performance.
