Contact Luck

How much more or less favorable a hitter's batted-ball outcomes were than what that contact was expected to produce. Runs per 100 eligible batted balls, {{ data_through_date[:4] }} season through {{ data_through_date_display }}.

What the number means

Contact Luck compares what a batted ball was expected to be worth with the run value of what actually happened. Summed across a player's eligible batted balls and normalized to Runs / 100, +3.0 means a hitter's realized outcomes were worth roughly three more runs per 100 eligible batted balls than expected. The interval shows the uncertainty around that estimate. Contact Luck describes what already happened. It is not a projection of future performance.

Ranked players only: {{ qualified_count }} of {{ player_count }} scored players qualify. The methodology page has the qualification rule, and how it works walks two real batted balls through end to end.
