Who’s getting the breaks?

Contact Luck compares what a batted ball was expected to be worth with the run value of what actually happened, summed across a player's eligible batted balls. A positive number means a hitter's realized outcomes were worth more runs than expected — a retrospective description of what happened, not a projection of future performance.
